Work of the association

Following the leadership transition on July 5, 2024, the board reported delays obtaining documents and financial records that affected legal, financial and operational work. Its published update lists replacement of community fencing, resolution of legal matters, assessment mailings, addressing violation concerns, managing taxes, development of the KMCA website and other essential community issues.

Mission & vision

The association’s mission is to preserve quality of life and a sense of community through effective management, administering its rules, bylaws and covenants, protecting property values and supporting community initiatives and capital improvements.

Its vision is a considerate, respectful and desirable community, with a high quality of life and connections among family, friends and neighbors.

Our values

Integrity. Fairness. Transparency. Community participation. Respect and cooperation. A strong sense of community.

Association objectives

  • Make responsible decisions as stewards of association funds and assets.
  • Uphold covenants, conditions, restrictions and rules fairly, ethically and objectively for all homeowners.
  • Respect concerns brought to the board and communicate resolutions verbally or in writing.
  • Provide accessible documents, newsletters, rules, assessment payment information and violation fines on the website.
  • Conduct regular neighborhood violation patrols.
  • Encourage volunteer participation and community service.
  • Evaluate issues against the association’s mission, vision and values rather than individual interests.
