About your payment receipt

When online payments are enabled, KMCA’s receipt service will send an association-branded receipt only after PayPal confirms a completed payment and the association’s payment record matches. A checkout return screen alone will never issue a receipt.

The receipt will show the payment amount, currency, date, property reference, and PayPal transaction ID. It confirms a payment received, not that an account is paid in full.
