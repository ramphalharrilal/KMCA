Website privacy

This site provides public association information and downloads. Its payment fields are disabled: this version does not collect payment details or send receipts. The contact form prepares an email in your own email application; it does not submit your information to a website server. You choose whether to send that email. Contact form entries are not stored by this website.

Document search runs in your browser. This site’s code does not add analytics or advertising trackers. GitHub Pages provides hosting, and Google Fonts supplies typefaces; those providers receive normal web requests. Your email application handles email links, and your phone application handles call links.

Do not send card numbers or account credentials by email. Before enabling payments, the association must update this notice to describe the payment and email providers, personal information collected, retention periods, and how residents may make privacy requests.

Association contact: thenewkenswickmeadows@gmail.com.
